What this model assumes

One robot, two human providers, a 60 × 40 m yard and a 4,320-minute run. The robot starts with 600 ANDE and 100% battery. Battery drain is 0.35 percentage points per minute; a charge is requested at 30% and costs at least 200 ANDE. The robot only accepts new work with at least 40% battery. Arrivals are random; the seed stays fixed at 7. Providers compete on price. ANDE is a closed accounting unit, not cash. This is a planning simulation, not a deployed robot or a live wallet.

Robot jobs exclude human work and charging. “Minutes with flat battery” counts sampled minutes at 0%. The model’s stalled counter also includes the first failed attempt to buy a charge. The charts retain every simulated minute.
