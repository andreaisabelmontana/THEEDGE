# ROBOPRENEUR preview media

The animated footage is the original RODEO laboratory demonstration, not an Andes field deployment.

- Source: https://github.com/IERoboticsAILab/RODEO/blob/c92d18750218388b02f6ab779c9dd7867c568d3f/assets/demo.gif
- Research: Milan Groshev and Eduardo Castelló Ferrer, RODEO: Robotic Decentralized Organization (ICRA 2026), https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.06058
- Copyright (c) 2026 Milan Groshev. MIT license reproduced in RODEO-LICENSE.txt.
- robopreneur.gif is the unmodified upstream 800 × 450, 12-second GIF.
- robopreneur-preview.mp4 is a silent H.264 encoding of the same 12 seconds, at 24 fps, for efficient browser playback.
- robopreneur-preview.webp is its first-frame fallback for no-JavaScript and reduced-motion visitors.

The website starts playback only when visible, pauses when hidden, and provides a pause button. The GIF is offered as a separate download.
