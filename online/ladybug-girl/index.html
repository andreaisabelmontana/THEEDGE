Ladybug Girl

A little character, a storybook meadow, and room to wander.

Focus
Character animation · Real-time 3D
Tools
Three.js · WebGL · Blender

01

The challenge

Make a rigged character feel at home in a small, explorable browser world.

02

The idea

A storybook meadow with a ladybug character, soft textures and simple movement controls.

03

The build

Three.js loads an animated GLB with walk and idle clips. Animation blending, ground contact and keyboard/touch controls connect movement to a cel-shaded, procedurally textured scene.

04

What came out of it

A playable third-person browser scene with walking, running, hopping and an orbiting camera.
