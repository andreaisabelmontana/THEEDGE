Robopreneur

A robot that does a job, gets paid for it, and buys the electricity it needs to keep going.

01

The challenge

Robots cost money to run, and the value they create lands on somebody else’s balance sheet. The ROBOPRENEUR line at my lab asks what happens if a robot earns and spends for itself.

02

The idea

Post the job with its payment already locked up. The robot does the work, and the money only moves once the evidence checks out.

03

The build

I wrote the task market that holds the payment, the service that reads the robot’s logs and decides whether to release it, the smart bin it serves, and a model of the site. It is planned for a collection yard in the Colombian Andes.

04

What came out of it

The model says the site needs about one job an hour before the robot covers its own charging. Below that it runs the battery flat and stops. Nothing has run on site yet, so that is a planning figure, not a result.
