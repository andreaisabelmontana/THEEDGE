MY IMPLEMENTATION

Robopreneur Andes

My separate implementation adapts the published RODEO and iTrash work for a proposed collection yard in the Colombian Andes. It includes a task market, a verifier, a smart-bin station and a model for deciding whether the site can support a robot.

01 / THE ECONOMIC LOOP

Work. Verify. Earn. Recharge.

Autonomy needs more than movement. It needs a way to agree on a job, prove it happened and keep the battery paid for.

  1. 01→

    Work

    A funded task is matched to an eligible provider. The robot travels, manipulates an item and records its actions.

  2. 02→

    Verify

    The oracle checks the committed proof: route, arm motion, correct disposal and work-area boundaries.

  3. 03→

    Earn

    Approval releases the agreed price. Any surplus returns to the customer; rejection returns the full escrow.

  4. 04→

    Recharge

    The robot spends its experimental credits on charging, then returns to work. Demand keeps the loop viable.

↺Energy makes the next job possible.

The blockchain records the agreement and the payment. The oracle remains a trusted service: a hash protects the submitted file from substitution, but does not prove that its physical claims are true.

02 / INSIDE THE ANDES BUILD

Four pieces. Two interfaces.

A proof bundle connects the robot and station to the verifier. A verdict connects the verifier to the escrow. Each component can be inspected independently.

02 / Python

Verification oracle

Checks a JSON Lines proof against a published policy. A mismatched digest or a failed check refuses payment with an explanation.

verdict.py L51–55
def judge(bundle: Bundle, policy: Policy, digest: str = "") -> Verdict:
    """Run a policy over a bundle."""
    results = tuple(policy.run(bundle))
    failures = [r for r in results if not r.passed]
    approved = not failures and bool(results)

What still needs to be proven

  • No Andes field deployment yet. The proposed protocol sets the run length, measurements and failure conditions in advance.
  • The model assumes open ground and straight-line travel. It excludes weather, hardware costs and real-world navigation uncertainty.
  • ANDE is a closed-loop experimental unit, not money or an investment. A positive balance does not establish commercial profitability.
  • One oracle holds the verification key. Evidence integrity is checked; the reliability of physical evidence still depends on the sensors and verifier.
