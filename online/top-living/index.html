Top Living

Designed and developed Top Living’s real estate website from interface design through implementation. Integrated the DOMUS API for property listings and search filters, added interactive maps, and built the PHP backend and frontend. The site is hosted on GoDaddy.

01

The challenge

Top Living manages its homes, offices and projects in Domus, a listings platform for agencies. The website had to show that inventory without anyone typing a property in twice, and turn visitors into conversations with an agent.

02

The idea

A site that reads listings directly from Domus, so what the team publishes there appears online on its own. Visitors search sales and rentals, open a detailed page for each property, and reach the agency through forms that land in its inbox.

03

The build

PHP on the Flight microframework, with Blade templates for every page. Guzzle calls the Domus API for listings and search filters, Leaflet places each property on a map, and PHPMailer sends the contact, property and “publish your property” forms to the team. Credentials live in environment variables, outside the code.

04

What came out of it

The site is live at topliving.com.co and has reached more than 11,000 users. Alongside it I researched the experience and designed a clickable mobile app prototype in Adobe XD.
