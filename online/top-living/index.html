01

The business need

Top Living needed a clear way for buyers and renters to explore available properties, narrow their search and reach the right person at the agency.

02

My role

As Software Engineer from April 2021 to April 2024, I translated business requirements into web improvements and managed deployments. I designed layouts in Adobe XD and implemented consistent styling across the PHP, JavaScript, HTML and CSS website.

03

The technical approach

I connected REST and DOMUS APIs to property search, listing pages and map-based filters, including Google Maps. Enquiry forms and automated lead-routing workflows connect prospective clients with the team. Location, price and property filters help visitors narrow their search. The live site is hosted on GoDaddy.

04

Why it matters

Supported Top Living’s 11,000+ active users through website improvements and deployment management. The property search and enquiry flow connect buyers and renters with the agency, while a consistent visual identity carries from Adobe XD layouts into the live site.
